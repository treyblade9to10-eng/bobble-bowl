Bobble Bowl sound credits
=========================
Every sound file in this folder was built from recordings that are public domain (CC0 1.0):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
CC0 means no permission or credit is needed; the sources are listed anyway so anyone can check them.
Freesound sounds were taken from their HQ preview files and cut, mixed, levelled and re-encoded to MP3.

MUSIC: theme.mp3 (title / menus), hub.mp3 (Season hub), sting_td.mp3, sting_kick.mp3
  Original composition written for Bobble Bowl (not based on any TV theme), sequenced in code and rendered offline.
  Instrument samples: VS Chamber Orchestra 2: Community Edition (VSCO 2 CE) by Versilian Studios
  (recorded by Sam Gossner & Simon Dalzell), license CC0 1.0
  https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE   (license file: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE/blob/master/LICENSE)
  Used: Trumpet (sus, stac), F Horn (sus, stac), Tenor Trombone (sus, stac), Tuba (sus, stac), Violin Section spiccato,
  Cello Section spiccato, Timpani hits + rolls, concert bass drum, marching snare + tenor drums (VSCO 1 Percussion),
  snare roll, crash cymbals, suspended cymbal hit + crescendo.
