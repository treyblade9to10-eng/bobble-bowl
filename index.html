How to Play

🏈 Offense

Pick a play, then hit SPACE to snap.

Move the QB with WASD / Arrows.

Throw with 1 2 3 4, or click a receiver.

Running with the ball: SHIFT = sprint, E = juke, F = spin.

🛡️ Defense

Pick a coverage, then the CPU snaps.

You control the player with the yellow ring.

Q = switch to the defender closest to the ball.

SPACE = dive tackle. SHIFT = sprint.

📱 Touch

Left stick moves. Tap a receiver to throw.

Buttons on the right do snap, sprint, juke and dive.
